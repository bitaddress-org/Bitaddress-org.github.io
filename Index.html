Identity & Liveness Verification

This identity and liveness verification is required as part of NOAH Foundation's grant screening and identity-verification process and is a precondition for proceeding with grant disbursement.

The purpose of this verification is to help confirm that the person completing the verification is the individual associated with the grant application.

What will be captured?

  • A live photograph captured directly through your device's front-facing camera.
  • Your face as it appears during the verification.
  • Your group/association ID card.
  • Information visibly displayed on the group/association ID card.
  • The verification photograph needed to document completion of the verification process.

Gallery uploads are not accepted. The verification photograph must be captured directly through the live camera.

How the information will be used

Information captured through this verification will be used solely for NOAH Foundation's grant screening, identity verification and related eligibility-assessment purposes.

NOAH Foundation is committed to using the information captured through this verification only for the purposes described above and will not intentionally use it for unrelated purposes.

The verification photograph is transmitted to NOAH Foundation's designated private storage account and is not intended to be publicly accessible through this website.

Before you begin

  • Have your group/association ID card with you.
  • Use a location with adequate lighting.
  • Allow your browser to access the front-facing camera when requested.
  • Keep your face and group/association ID card clearly visible.

Complete Verification

Hold your group/association ID card at approximately chest or neck level and keep your face visible.

Keep your face inside the upper guide and your group/association ID card inside the lower guide.
